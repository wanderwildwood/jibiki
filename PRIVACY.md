# Dictionary — Privacy Policy

Version 0.1.6. Last reviewed 23 October 2026.

## The short version

Dictionary collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. It has no internet permission, and no permission of any kind, so no data can leave your phone even in principle. The whole word list is inside the app. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The developer never receives anything about you or your use of the app, and has no way to.

## Who is responsible

wander wildwood, the author of the app. Contact: email@wanderthe.dev

Because no personal data is transmitted to or held by the developer, there is no data controller relationship for the developer to administer. Where the app processes personal data, that processing happens on your own device, under your control.

## What the app stores, and where

In its own private storage on the phone: a copy of the word list, unpacked from the app on first use, and the last 30 words you looked up, so the search field can offer them again (the "Clear this list" line in the search screen removes them). Nothing else. The app is left out of Android's backups (`allowBackup="false"`), the word list sits in a folder Android does not back up, and uninstalling the app removes all of it. There is no cloud copy and no copy held by the developer.

The word list is Open English WordNet 2025 (CC BY 4.0), bundled in the app. Looking a word up reads that file on the phone and talks to nothing.

## Define, and the text you select

Define appears in the menu over text selected in another app. When you choose it, Android hands this app the selected text, and only that text, for the lookup. If the selection came from a field you can edit and you press a word to swap it in, the app hands that word back to the app you came from, which puts it in the field. The app does not read the rest of the screen or the field, and does not keep the selection beyond the last-30 list above.

## Permissions, and what each is for

None. The manifest has no `uses-permission` line. It carries one `queries` entry (apps that offer a text action), so that the search field's selection menu can list them behind its ⋮; it grants no access to anything on the phone.

## Who else receives anything

No one. The app contacts no host.

In About, "feed the llamas" opens a donation page (square.link) and wanderthe.dev opens the author's website, in your browser and only when you press them. The app does not fetch either page and learns nothing about whether you went.

No data is sold, rented, or shared. There is no profiling, no personalization, and no automated decision-making about you.

## Legal basis (EU / EEA and UK)

No personal data is collected, transmitted or processed by the developer, so no legal basis under Article 6 GDPR is engaged on the developer's part. Processing that happens wholly on your own device, for your own purposes, is outside the scope of the Regulation (Article 2(2)(c)).

## Your rights

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Under the CCPA/CPRA and comparable North American laws you have rights to know, delete, correct and opt out of sale or sharing.

The developer holds no data about you, so there is nothing to request from them, and nothing has ever been sold or shared. You can exercise every one of these rights directly: the data is on your phone. Clear the app's data in Android's settings, or uninstall to remove all of it at once.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect anything from anyone, of any age.

## Changes

If a later version changes what the app stores or starts contacting anything, this policy is updated in the same release and the change is described in the release notes.
